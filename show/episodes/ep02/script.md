*Rendered Rants. A vlog, filmed upright: JAMES films himself on his phone, propped on
the edge of the bath, on the dash mount in the estate car, hand-held down the office
corridor, and propped up at his desk, on the sofa, in the kitchen and at his gaming PC.
All lines are James's. Rebuilt from episode 1 of his cartoon vlog ("12 months of AI - A
Personal story" by James), same words; in the bath he is `james_bath`.*

**Title card:** 12 Months of AI

---

**THE BATH, NIGHT.** JAMES in a bubble bath, the phone propped on the edge of the tub. Candles and a rubber duck on the shelf behind him.

JAMES (cheerful, slightly sheepish, straight to camera): Hiya. Yes, I'm in the bath. Stay with me, there's a point to this.
JAMES (wry, savouring the word): Twelve months ago, my daily routine was distinctly... terrestrial.
JAMES (listing them, a bit weary): Frantic typing of notes. The heavy lifting of mundane admin.
JAMES (softer, a little wistful): And the quiet feeling I had some very large thoughts, with nowhere to put them.
JAMES (leaning in, conspiratorial, a nod): Today, there's an invisible co-pilot, quietly rewriting my entire operating system.
JAMES (thoughtful; the phone tilts): It's a subtle shift. But laid out end to end, it borders on the surreal.
JAMES (brisk, setting off): Let me show you. It starts on the morning commute.

**THE CAR, MORNING.** The phone on the dash mount. James in the driver's seat; the empty passenger seat beside him.

JAMES (matter of fact): A year ago, the drive to the office was dead time.
JAMES (remembering the stress): Then a mad scramble to prep for meetings at my desk.
JAMES (pleased with himself): Now? The car is a mobile strategy room.
JAMES (casual, demonstrating): I just talk to my phone. The rough edges of a project, out loud.
JAMES (building): And by the time I park, the research is done,
JAMES (landing it, quietly smug, a nod): and the diagrams I need are already drawn.

JAMES (upbeat, walking): So I walk into the building,
JAMES (triumphant, a little cheeky; the phone tilts): with three hours of work already finished.
JAMES (confident): And that momentum? It bleeds into the rest of the day.

**THE DESK.** The phone propped against the keyboard. Beside James, his monitor shows a 47-message "RE: RE: FW:" email chain.

JAMES (delighted, a little smug, a nod): and it's three bullet points. Before my coffee has even cooled.

**THE SOFA, EVENING.** The phone propped up on the sofa, the record shelves behind him.

JAMES (relaxed, evening voice): Then the workday ends, and the real magic takes over my private life.
JAMES (deadpan): It starts with the mundane. A digital bill lands in my inbox.
JAMES (grimacing slightly, shaking his head): The kind of life admin that used to just sit there, nagging at me.
JAMES (breezy, like giving an order to a butler): Now I just tell the agent, file it in the right Drive folder.

JAMES (amused, reading it out): Later, a friend messages. Can I help design a promotional flyer?
JAMES (rueful): That used to mean sacrificing a perfectly good Tuesday evening,
JAMES (exasperated, then trailing off; a facepalm): wrestling with layout software. And fonts. So many fonts.
JAMES (casual, pleased, glancing at the kettle): Instead, I describe the vibe to the agent while the kettle boils.
JAMES (building): Twenty minutes. And by the time I sit down,
JAMES (landing it): we've got a polished, print-ready design.

**THE GAMING DEN, NIGHT.** James in his red gaming chair; the factory game glows on the monitor beside him.

JAMES (lower, late-night voice): Even my downtime has been rewired.
JAMES (geeky enthusiasm, then frustration, looking at the screen): I fire up a sprawling factory-building game, and hit a wall on resource routing.
JAMES (mock horror; the phone tilts): Old me? An hour trawling archaic wiki pages and forums.
JAMES (warm, matter of fact): Now I just ask my agent. It knows my playstyle. It knows the game.

**THE BATH.** Closer.
